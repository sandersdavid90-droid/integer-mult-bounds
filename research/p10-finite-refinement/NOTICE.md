This is a finite arithmetic refinement and reproduction of eumemic's PR317,
not a new circuit mechanism. Its immutable source is
`0a0b61a7f4601a96036af8bb6a6338c58f035d2e`. No upstream files are copied or
modified. The external package is checked against its exact manifest.

PR317, prepared with OpenAI Codex assistance, supplies the composed physical
word, kernel/target/retiming/restoration/reordering selections, complete
admission, bank realization and finite invoice. DreamingOfClouds's PR315,
prepared with Anthropic Claude assistance, supplies the p=10 circuit and
centre-sharing complex supplier.

The finite-level and balanced-assembly techniques are inherited community
work, including earlier finite refinements such as PR243, PR252 and PR303.
The new contribution is their explicitly source-bound application to PR317,
an independently written rational moment checker, a finer root bracket,
eight explicit positive-gap finite cutoffs, and a bound on remaining
arithmetic headroom for this fixed profile and assembly.
